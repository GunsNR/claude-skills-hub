South Florida Impact Window & Door SEO

Data-driven niche selection for the RankLogic agency site, validated against Semrush keyword volume, CPC, competition, and Keyword Difficulty Index (KDI). The pick: residential impact window & hurricane door installers in South Florida (Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach).

Why this niche, in one line: CPC of $22–$30 on the head terms proves the lead is high-ticket; KDI of 0–28 on neighborhood & near-me terms proves it ranks fast; a regulatory tailwind (Florida Building Code + active My Safe Florida Home grant) means demand is climbing into 2026.

1 · The Pick

One agency target: Residential impact window & hurricane door installation companies across South Florida. Job value $15k–$50k. Gross margin 25–40%. One closed deal covers 5 months of a $3,500/mo agency retainer.

One geo: Miami-Dade + Broward + Palm Beach counties. ~30 city/neighborhood slugs to own.

One promise: Page 1 in 90 days on the city & neighborhood cluster, or refund. impact windows miami itself is a 6–9 month stretch goal, not a 90-day commit.

8 · On-Page SEO Standards (apply globally)

  • H1 contains primary keyword and the geo (one per page).
  • Title tag ≤ 65 chars. Description tag 150–158 chars.
  • Canonical set on every route.
  • Organization JSON-LD on /, Service on /pricing and city pages, FAQPage on / and city pages.
  • Image alts: descriptive, include city or keyword where natural — never stuffed.
  • OG + Twitter Card metadata on every route via generatePageMeta().
  • Sitemap auto-includes all routes (app/sitemap.ts).
  • Core Web Vitals: LCP < 2.0s, CLS < 0.05. Use Next/Image, font-display swap (already set).
