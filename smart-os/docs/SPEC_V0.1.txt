============================================================
1. PRODUCT DEFINITION
============================================================

Smart OS / AI OS is a safe, local-first personal AI command center.

Its purpose is to centralize the user's projects, tools, local files, workflows, memory, models, automations, credentials, approvals, observability, and growth intelligence into one controlled operating layer.

The system is designed to let the user ask AI to understand, plan, draft, propose, compare, route, evaluate, and coordinate work across real projects and AI tools while keeping risky actions behind approval gates.

Core product promise:
AI that can safely work across your tools, projects, memory, and workflows without giving up control.

Primary category:
Safe AI command center.

Secondary categories:
- Personal AI OS
- AI operating layer
- AI control plane
- AI workflow automation system
- Local-first AI assistant
- AI project command center
- Agent orchestration dashboard

Primary user:
A power user, developer, founder, operator, or creator who wants one local-first control center for projects, notes, coding agents, AI models, automations, SEO/growth work, and workflows.
