## Link ideas that fit this business

1. **Industry bodies and suppliers.** Window and door manufacturers, trade associations, and local chambers sometimes list contractors or vendors. Check each group's membership and listing rules before applying. Do not assume any of them link back.
2. **Local Fort Lauderdale and Broward sources.** Greater Fort Lauderdale chambers, local business journals, and community groups. A real sponsorship or event can earn a link.
3. **A shareable resource.** The Google Business Profile checklist post can be offered as a downloadable PDF. Homeowner-facing sites will not link to it, but contractor associations and local business blogs might.
4. **Sister-company mentions.** If RankLogic SEO already has editorial mentions or profiles, ask for a relevant, natural link to Impact Windows SEO. Keep it editorial and sparing.
5. **Reporter requests.** Services that connect journalists with sources reward concrete, original expertise. Answer only questions you can answer honestly.
