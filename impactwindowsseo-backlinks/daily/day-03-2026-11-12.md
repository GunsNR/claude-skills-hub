## 1. Yellow Pages

- **Start:** yellowpages.com. Search for "Impact Windows SEO" in Fort Lauderdale first. If a listing exists, guides describe a "Claim This Business" link that leads to accounts.yellowpages.com. If none exists, guides describe a "Get Your Free Listing" path.
- **Verification:** guides say YP calls the phone number on the listing with a short code. Have (954) 706-6785 ready.
- **Honest warning:** Local Search Forum users report free listings that say they will publish in 48 hours and never do, and say Thryv (which runs Yellow Pages) pushes paid services. If it stalls, note it in the log and move on.
- **Do not:** buy a Thryv or YP paid package as part of this task.
- **Not confirmed:** cost on YP's own pages, website-link attributes, support phone numbers (forum-reported numbers were inconsistent, so none are listed here).

## 2. Nextdoor Business Page

- **Start:** business.nextdoor.com (official page opened: "Claim your free Business Page").
- **Who can claim:** an owner, employee, or other authorized representative (official page).
- **Verification:** per Nextdoor's help content in search results, claiming an existing page with a phone number on file allows phone call or text verification; a new page may require business documents from account settings.
- **What you can add:** business information, contact details, photos (official page). Whether a website link field exists and how it is tagged was not confirmed.
- **Note:** the business page is tied to a personal Nextdoor account.
- **Have ready:** a personal Nextdoor account, exact NAP, short description, real photos.

## 3. Foursquare for Business

- **Start:** Foursquare's business portal. Official help article (opened): https://support.foursquare.com/hc/en-us/articles/23646714300444-Claiming-a-listing. Search for the business, click **Claim** on the matching listing, complete the claim form. If no listing exists, guides say you can create one.
- **Verification:** the official article only says the form confirms your identity and role. Third-party guides list phone, postcard, or credit card options.
- **Cost:** the official article does not mention a fee. One guide (Chatmeter) reports an optional one-time $20 fee for instant access. Unconfirmed. Use the free route if it is offered.
- **Not confirmed:** website field and link attributes.

## 4. DesignRush

- **Start:** https://www.designrush.com/add-listing (opened; redirects to the agency submission form). Required to start: first name, last name, email, phone, password.
- **Review:** the form page says DesignRush only accepts agencies it considers legitimate and professional, and reviews the website, portfolio, online reputation, and reviews before publishing (opened). Acceptance is not assured, especially for a newer agency without a portfolio.
- **Cost:** DesignRush pages reference a free listing; the submission form itself does not state a price. Sponsorship packages are paid and are not part of this task.
- **Do not:** invent a portfolio, client list, or case study. Note from search results: one account can hold one agency profile, and profiles reportedly cannot be deleted.

## 5. Trustpilot Business

- **Start:** https://business.trustpilot.com/signup (opened; page title "Create Your Free Account"). The form asks for business location, business name, and business website.
- **Verification:** Trustpilot's developer docs mention a "Verify your domain" step; the exact methods (work email, file, DNS) were not confirmed. An @impactwindowsseo.com email will likely make this easier.
- **Caution:** Trustpilot is a review platform. Do not invite reviews with any incentive, and only ask real clients. With no reviews, the page will look empty; that is fine.
