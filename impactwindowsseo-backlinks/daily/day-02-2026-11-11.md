## 1. Yelp for Business

- **Start:** biz.yelp.com (confirm the address; I could not open it). Search for "Impact Windows SEO" first. If a page exists and is marked unclaimed, use "Claim this business." If none exists, add a new business.
- **What guides say:** claiming and managing a basic page is free. You create a Yelp business account, enter category, phone, website, and address, then verify. Verification is usually an automated call to the business phone with a code. Some claims go to manual review, which guides say can take a few business days.
- **Website field:** guides show a step to add the business website. Link attributes not checked.
- **Watch out:** Yelp is widely reported to discourage businesses from asking customers for reviews at all. Do not add Yelp to review request messages. Do not buy Yelp ads to "boost" a listing unless you decide that separately.
- **Have ready:** access to (954) 706-6785 for the verification call, a business email.

## 2. LinkedIn Company Page

- **Start:** from a personal LinkedIn account, open the Work (grid) menu, then "Create a Company Page." LinkedIn help: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/100826
- **What sources say:** the page is free. LinkedIn's help page says to confirm the email address on the personal account first. Third-party guides say LinkedIn may ask the creator to verify their workplace, with a company-domain email (not Gmail or Yahoo) or a Microsoft Entra Verified ID, and that very new personal accounts can be blocked. Those last details came from blogs and conflicted, so treat them as unconfirmed.
- **Have ready:** a personal LinkedIn account in good standing, an @impactwindowsseo.com email if one exists, logo, the short description from `business-profile.txt`, website URL, Fort Lauderdale location.
- **Do not:** list employee counts, founding dates, or specialties you have not confirmed.

## 3. Facebook Business Page

- **Start:** facebook.com/pages/create (confirm the address; I could not open it), signed in with a personal profile.
- **What guides say:** a personal profile is required to create a Page, but the Page is separate and your personal details are not shown on it. Setup asks for page name, category, and a short bio. Website, phone, and address are added in the page details after creation. One guide says a Page can also be created through Meta's business tools instead of from a personal profile.
- **Not confirmed:** whether Meta Business Verification is needed or useful for a page like this. None of the sources covered it. It is not required just to create a Page, as far as the sources say.
- **Have ready:** logo and cover image (your own, not stock if possible), the medium description, exact NAP.

## 4. Manta

- **Start:** manta.com, then "Add Business" in the header. That flow also checks whether a listing already exists; if it does, choose "This is my company" to claim it. (From a third-party guide; confirm on Manta.)
- **Cost:** a free listing exists. Manta also sells paid plans. One third-party page quoted $49 a month for a plan with verified listings and review monitoring. I did not confirm that price. A free listing is enough.
- **Verification:** I could not find how Manta verifies ownership. Expect an email or phone step.
- **Honest note:** one commentary argued directories like Manta matter less now that Google dominates local search. It is a low-effort, low-risk listing, not a big win.
- **Do not:** buy the paid plan as part of this task.

## 5. Greater Fort Lauderdale Chamber of Commerce

- **Start:** membership application at https://business.ftlchamber.com/application (site: ftlchamber.com). Phone (954) 462-6000. Address 512 NE 3rd Avenue, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33301 (from the chamber's own contact listing, via search).
- **Cost:** membership is paid. I could not find current dues. Call or check the application page before deciding.
- **Listing:** the application page says the primary account user can edit the listing details and upload a logo. I could not confirm whether the member directory includes a website link or how it is tagged. Ask when you call.
- **Don't confuse:** the Greater Fort Lauderdale LGBT Chamber of Commerce is a separate organization.
- **Decision for the owner:** this costs money. Only join if the chamber's events and network are worth it on their own; the listing alone is not a reason.

## Considered and not used today

- **Better Business Bureau.** Accreditation is paid, fees vary by chapter and business size, and BBB's standards include about six months of active selling plus proof of licensing. I could not confirm whether a free, non-accredited profile can be requested, or whether the business meets the six-month rule. Revisit with the owner.
- **UpCity.** A free basic profile appears to exist, and UpCity says it checks location against the Google Business Profile. Search results showed UpCity pages mirrored on another domain, and I could not confirm the current signup flow. Revisit.
