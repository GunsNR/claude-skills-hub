## 1. Sortlist

- **Start:** sortlist.com, provider registration (help center points to the providers page). Official listing conditions (opened): https://intercom.help/sortlist/en/articles/1714392-can-i-register-to-sortlist-listing-conditions
- **What the help center says:** creating a provider profile is free; services must fit at least one category (Advertising & Marketing agencies is one); the profile must be live; freelancers and single-employee firms are allowed if stated. It also says Sortlist's marketing focuses on Benelux, French, and Spanish markets, so US buyer traffic may be limited.
- **Not confirmed:** verification steps and timeline, website link attributes. An older help page mentions a weekly onboarding webinar.
- **Do not:** add portfolio items or client reviews that are not real. Do not buy Sortlist+ as part of this task.

## 2. Hotfrog

- **Start:** hotfrog.com, "Add Business." Official steps (opened): https://support.hotfrog.com/getting-started/how-to-add-your-business-to-hotfrog
- **Steps from the official page:** register with an email, confirm via the emailed link, set a password, enter company name and address and pin the map, submit, then click "Verify now" to verify ownership.
- **Cost:** the official page does not state it. Third-party guides say a free basic profile exists and to choose the basic option.
- **Not confirmed:** website field and link attributes.

## 3. Brownbook

- **Start:** brownbook.net, "Add a business" (from BrightLocal and AdviceLocal guides; Brownbook's own page not opened).
- **Process from guides:** register, confirm by email link, add the business or claim an existing listing for free.
- **Cost:** guides describe a free tier with paid upgrades. Use the free tier.
- **Not confirmed:** anything on Brownbook's own pages. Treat all steps as unverified until you see them.
