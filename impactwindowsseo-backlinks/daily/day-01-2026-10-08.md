## 1. GoodFirms

- **Start:** https://www.goodfirms.co/goodfirms-pro, then "Apply for Free Listing."
- **What the page says:** free tier with no payment required; applications are reviewed through a verification process and acceptance is not guaranteed. A search summary of the page quoted a 23% acceptance rate for free listings. Treat that figure as unconfirmed until you see it on the page.
- **Have ready:** company overview, services, industries served, team size, pricing model, location, website. Complete profiles are said to rank better in their lists.
- **Paid tier:** a verified-business badge sits behind a paid plan. Skip it for now.
- **Do not:** invent a portfolio, case study, or client reviews. Add them only when real.

## 2. Clutch

- **Start:** https://clutch.co/get-listed
- **What the help center says:** create a free company profile in three steps; sign in with LinkedIn, Google, or a company email; fill in company name, tagline, headcount, minimum project size, average hourly rate, website, locations, contact info, overview, and service focus. The profile goes to Clutch's team for review before it is published.
- **Reviews:** Clutch verifies client reviews itself, either by interviewing the client or through its online form. Only ask real clients, and do not offer anything in exchange.
- **Have ready:** the fields above. If you are not sure of an hourly rate or minimum project size, enter only what you can stand behind.

## 3. Crunchbase

- **Start:** log in or register, search first to make sure the company is not already listed, then Resources, Create Profile, choose organization. Official help: https://support.crunchbase.com/hc/en-us/articles/115011823988
- **Caveat:** Crunchbase's help pages say contributions should focus on the tech ecosystem and that profiles outside it are likely to be removed. A local SEO agency may or may not qualify. Expect that it could be removed.
- **Control:** verifying your employment gives you control over parts of the overview.
- **Have ready:** logo, founded date, website, headquarters (Fort Lauderdale, FL), short description, industries.

## 4. Bing Places

- **Start:** https://www.bingplaces.com (confirm the address; I could not open it). Sign in with a Microsoft account.
- **Process (from third-party guides):** search by phone or name first to avoid creating a duplicate; claim a match or create a new business; enter name and address exactly as on official documents; verify by one of the offered methods (postcard, phone or email PIN, others vary).
- **Cost:** the sources I found did not say whether it is free. Check Microsoft's help pages before you start.
- **Use:** exact NAP from `../business-profile.txt`.

## 5. Apple Business Connect

- **Start:** https://businessconnect.apple.com (confirm the address; I could not open it). Sign in with an Apple ID.
- **Process (from third-party guides, Apple's own page not opened):** search for the business or add a new location, confirm the map pin, add hours, category, website, phone, then submit company details and an identifiable business document for verification. Customization unlocks after Apple verifies.
- **Cost:** guides describe it as free.
